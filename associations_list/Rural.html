Rural Development Association (RDA)

About the association

The Rural Development Association of St. Joseph Church, Kalimar, is dedicated to promoting the social and economic development of the local community. The association seeks to improve the quality of life of people, especially those from rural and underprivileged backgrounds, by encouraging awareness, education, cooperation, and community participation. Through various development initiatives and social welfare activities, it strives to create opportunities for a better future and build a society based on equality, dignity, and mutual support.

Mission & Vision

Mission

The mission of the Rural Development Association is to support the development of rural communities by promoting education, social awareness, self-reliance, and collective responsibility. It aims to identify the needs of the community, encourage participation in development activities, and provide support to individuals and families facing social and economic challenges.

Vision

The association envisions a well-developed and self-reliant community where every individual has access to opportunities for growth, education, and a dignified life. It strives to build a society founded on equality, cooperation, social responsibility, and sustainable development.
