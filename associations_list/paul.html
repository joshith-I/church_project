Society of St. Vincent de Paul (SSVP)

About the association

The Society of St. Vincent de Paul at St. Joseph Church, Kalimar, is a Catholic charitable organization inspired by the teachings of Jesus Christ and the example of St. Vincent de Paul. The association is dedicated to serving the poor, needy, and vulnerable members of society with love, compassion, and humility. Through charitable assistance, personal visits, and acts of kindness, its members strive to bring hope, comfort, and dignity to those facing difficulties. The society encourages its members to recognize Christ in every person in need and to express their faith through selfless service.

Mission & Vision

Mission

The mission of the Society of St. Vincent de Paul is to provide compassionate and practical assistance to individuals and families experiencing poverty and hardship. Through charitable works, personal care, and spiritual support, the society seeks to relieve suffering, uphold human dignity, and extend Christian love to those in need, without discrimination.

Vision

The society envisions a compassionate and caring community where no person is forgotten or left without support. It strives to build a society rooted in charity, solidarity, and Christian brotherhood, where people willingly share their resources and extend a helping hand to those experiencing hardship.
