Our Patron

St. Joseph is honoured by the Church as our patron and as the father and guardian of families. As the foster father of Jesus Christ, he lived a life full of love and sacrifice. His life of humility, honesty and hard work stands as a model for everyone. Obedient to the will of God, he lovingly protected and guided the Holy Family. We pray that St. Joseph, our patron, will always protect our families and our parish community with his grace. Through his intercession, may God grant us the grace to remain steadfast in faith, peace and love of God. We pray that the fatherly love and protection of St. Joseph may abide in our homes forever. By his blessing, may the people of our parish grow and flourish in unity and devotion. May the life of St. Joseph shine in our hearts forever as a light of simplicity, peace and dedication. Through his intercession, may our young people grow in good deeds and receive the grace to walk firmly in the way of God.
