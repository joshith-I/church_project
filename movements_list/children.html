Children Movement

About the movement

The Children's Movement of St. Joseph Church, Kalimar, seeks to provide children with an environment where they can grow in faith, learn Christian values, and develop a spirit of love, service, and responsibility. Through appropriate faith formation, fellowship, and activities, the movement encourages children to discover the joy of knowing Christ and to become active members of the Church from an early age.

Mission & Vision

Mission

The mission of the Children's Movement is to nurture children in Christian faith and values while helping them develop positive attitudes towards God, others, and society. The movement aims to encourage children to learn, participate, share, and grow together as young members of the parish community.

Vision

The Children's Movement envisions a generation of children who grow with a strong foundation in faith, compassion, discipline, and love for others. It seeks to help children develop into responsible and faithful members of the Church and society.
