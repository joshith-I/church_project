Young Christian Students(YCS) Movement

About the movement

The Young Christian Students (YCS) of St. Joseph Church, Kalimar, is a movement that brings together young Christian students of the parish to grow in faith, develop a sense of responsibility, and respond positively to the needs of others. Through fellowship, awareness, and service, YCS encourages students to live their Christian values in their daily lives and to contribute meaningfully to their parish, school, and community.

Mission & Vision

Mission

The mission of YCS is to encourage young Christian students to understand their responsibilities towards the Church and society. The movement seeks to provide assistance to those in need, create awareness among students and the wider community, and encourage young people to put their faith into action through service and responsible participation.

Vision

YCS envisions a community of young Christian students who are faithful, socially responsible, compassionate, and committed to making a positive difference in the lives of others. The movement seeks to help students grow not only in their academic and personal lives, but also in their Christian values and sense of service.
