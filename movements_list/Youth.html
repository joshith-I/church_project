Youth Movement

About the movement

The Youth Movement of St. Joseph Church, Kalimar, is a community of young people committed to growing in faith, serving others, and developing themselves as responsible Christian leaders. Through regular gatherings and activities, the movement encourages young people to deepen their relationship with Christ while actively participating in the life and mission of the parish. The movement seeks to create an environment where young people can learn, serve, support one another, and use their talents for the good of the church and society.

Mission & Vision

Mission

The mission of the Youth Movement is to nurture young people in Christian faith and values while helping them develop positive attitudes towards God, others, and society.

Vision

The Youth Movement envisions a generation of young adults who grow with a strong foundation in faith, compassion, discipline, and love for others.
