Gnannamaal Women's Movement

About the movement

The Women's Movement of St. Joseph Church, Kalimar, is a community of women united by their faith in Christ and their commitment to the spiritual and social development of the parish. The movement encourages women to grow in Christian values, strengthen their fellowship, and actively participate in the mission of the Church. Through prayer, mutual support, charitable service, and various community activities, it seeks to empower women to become responsible, compassionate, and active contributors to the Church and society.

Mission & Vision

Mission

The mission of the Women's Movement is to strengthen the faith and fellowship of women while encouraging them to live according to Christian values. It strives to promote unity, support families, extend assistance to the poor and needy, and actively participate in parish and community development through acts of love, compassion, and service.

Vision

The Women's Movement envisions a community of faithful, confident, and compassionate women who serve as examples of Christian love and dedication. It aims to nurture women who contribute positively to their families, Church, and society while inspiring future generations through faith, unity, leadership, and service.
